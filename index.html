Data sources. Risk-factor rates come from the CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index for Census Tract 215.17, Denton County, TX (65+, no vehicle, limited English, income below 150% of poverty, disability, under 18). The tract is larger than Meridian, so it is an approximation. Households, damage and the other factors are synthetic. Street layout, house positions and surrounding terrain (lake, creek, woods, park) are traced from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors), so they are approximate.

Risk only changes who is helped first. It never changes coverage or claim decisions, and people make the final call.
