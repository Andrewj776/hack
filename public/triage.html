After a disaster, thousands of texts, calls and claims arrive at once. An AI model reads each one, pulls out the need type and urgency, and explains its ranking in plain words. A person reviews the list and makes every decision.

    All messages are made up. Rankings were produced ahead of time by an LLM and checked by a person, so nothing here calls an AI live. The AI only suggests an order. It never changes coverage or claim decisions. Real use would need customer consent and local responder coordination.
